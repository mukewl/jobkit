# Set up jobkit for this person

Follow **`BOOTSTRAP.md`** at the repo root. It is the full script and the single source of
truth for onboarding — read it now and work through its phases in order.

Three things that matter more than the rest, so they are repeated here:

**Six questions, no more.** Their CV carries their name, contact details, history,
seniority, skills and often their languages. Read it and extract all of that. Asking someone
to retype what you are already holding is what makes setup feel like paperwork.

**Show your work once, then go.** After the questions, print one compact summary of
everything you extracted and everything they told you, and ask a single "anything wrong?".
Not a confirmation per field.

**Finish on a working example.** The setup is not done when the files are written. It is
done when a real job they chose has produced a one-page PDF and a list of contacts. A
configured pipeline with nothing in it is a failed setup, and they will not find out until
tomorrow.

If anything in `BOOTSTRAP.md` cannot be completed — a missing dependency they decline to
install, a job board with no usable export — say so plainly at hand-over and tell them what
still needs doing. Do not report success over a gap.
